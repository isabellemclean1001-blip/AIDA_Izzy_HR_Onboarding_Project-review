# AIDA 1141 Project Exercise 01 – Workflow Question

## Use-case question
Can a model identify at least 90% of synthetic task assessments that would miss the employee's start date without intervention, while keeping false alerts manageable and performing better than simple follow-up rules?

## Intended staff decision
The HR onboarding coordinator at the fictional Town of Pinecrest confirms the responsible team and reviews the model's risk suggestion. The coordinator decides whether to follow up, expedite the task, or continue normally, and confirms completion once the responsible team reports that the work is done. 

## Steps completed
1. Defined the fictional staff user and the onboarding workflow's scope.
2. Drafted the problem brief, including the staff decision, measurable question, proposed success measure, and exclusions. 
3. Created a Mermaid workflow diagram showing information checks, routing, risk assessments, human decisions, and completion tracking. 

## Result
This exercise produced a problem brief and a workflow diagram describing the proposed onboarding routing and follow-up processes.

- [Problem brief](problem-brief.md)
- [Workflow diagram](workflow-diagram.md)

## What the result means
The workflow, staff responsibilities, and proposed model evaluation are now defined. The next exercise will use this framing to create synthetic request and assessment records, a data dictionary, and a data card. 

## Limitation
The workflow is simplified, and the model will be evaluated using simulated outcomes rather than real onboarding records. Its results will depend on the assumptions built into the generator and cannot establish effectiveness in a real HR department. 

## Human reviewer action
Review the request types, responsible team, and assessment checkpoints to confirm that they are reasonable and consistent with the problem brief.
