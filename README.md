# Individual HR Onboarding Project

This private repository is the working space for Izzy's individual project-building exercises and integrated project development across AIDA 1141, AIDA 2156, and AIDA 2154.

## Working question

Could a carefully evaluated digital intake workflow help a fictional HR team route and track onboarding requests?

Keep the project to one onboarding intake-and-routing workflow for a fictional HR team lead. Use synthetic tabular records and computer-generated, non-personal images only. Do not use real employee, City of Red Deer, employer, client, or confidential records. Screen mockups may explain the workflow; a production application is not required. A person must review any suggested category or action.

## Three course strands

- **AIDA 1141:** Analyze whether structured synthetic records can help identify requests that may need staff follow-up.
- **AIDA 2156:** Explore where delays or incomplete handoffs appear and how reliable those findings are.
- **AIDA 2154:** Test a bounded computer-vision method on generated, non-personal form illustrations.

The individual project-building exercises are formative steps. They do not replace the three separate final projects, each worth 25%, or the regular labs and knowledge checks in any course. Follow each D2L assignment for its required work and submission.
